Read with care

  • 2026 election results are unofficial until states certify.
  • Nothing here is an estimate. The maps and charts describe where things happened and how the vote moved.
  • Swing is the change in the Republican two-party share from the 2024 presidential race to the 2026 Senate race, or the governor's race where the Senate race was not Democrat against Republican. Counties in states with neither race have no swing.
  • A site's date is when it entered the FracTracker list, not when it was announced. Days when the list logged 30 or more sites at once are marked on the timeline as catch-up batches.
  • Opposition actions count datacentertracker.org, Moratorium Nation and the Crowd Counting Consortium. A moratorium logged by two sources counts once.
  • The state-by-state comparison sets counties with at least one FracTracker site against their neighbors in the same state that have none, so each row compares one race. Only states with five or more data center counties are shown. The data center counties were less Republican to start with.
  • House: of the 230 seats Republicans held before the election, 197 have a declared winner. The 33 still being counted are drawn dashed on the map and set aside in the fight comparison.
  • Issue codes come from a language-model classifier and are exploratory.
